Our Story

From a single vision in 2025 to a nationwide community of chai lovers, our journey has been fueled by a passion for authentic Indian chai culture.

Founded on a Dream

Sharma Ji Ki Chai was born in 2025 from a simple idea: to create a space where people could connect over a genuinely good cup of chai. We wanted to revive the tradition of "chai pe charcha" (discussions over tea) and make it accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Mission & Values

  • Authenticity: Serving real, freshly-brewed chai.
  • Community: Building spaces for connection.
  • Quality: Sourcing the finest tea leaves and spices.
  • Hospitality: Treating every guest like family.

The Sharma Ji Ki Chai Experience

Step into any of our cafes and you're greeted by more than just the aroma of brewing spices. It's a feeling of comfort, a pause from the rush of daily life. We've created a space where the warmth of the chai is matched only by the warmth of the conversations around you. It's a sensory journey—the clink of glasses, the scent of fresh ginger, and the shared laughter of friends.

Our Commitment to Quality

A perfect cup of chai starts with the finest ingredients. We travel to the lush tea gardens of Assam and Darjeeling to source single-origin leaves. Our signature masala is a secret family recipe, with spices like cardamom, cloves, and cinnamon freshly ground in-house every day. This dedication ensures that every sip you take is authentic, aromatic, and absolutely perfect.

More Than Just a Cafe

We believe a chai cafe should be the heartbeat of its community. Our spaces are designed to be hubs of connection and creativity. From hosting open mic nights and local art exhibitions to simply being a place where new ideas are born over a cup of tea, we are proud to be a part of your stories, your friendships, and your community's culture.

What We Provide: A Partnership for Success

Site Selection & Build-Out

We provide expert guidance in identifying the perfect location and assistance with cafe design and construction to recreate the signature Sharma Ji Ki Chai ambiance.

Comprehensive Training

Marketing & Brand Support

You'll benefit from our national brand campaigns and receive a toolkit of local marketing materials to attract and retain a loyal customer base.

Supply Chain

Ongoing Operational Support

Technology & Systems

Leverage our modern POS systems and operational technology to manage sales, inventory, and customer relationships with ease.
